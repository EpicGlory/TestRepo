Our Story

Born from the Pit

A Utah BBQ company built on late nights, great fire, and an obsession with flavor.

How It Started

From Backyard to Brand

Cosmic Pig BBQ started the way all great things do — with a fire, a piece of meat, and the nagging feeling that something could taste better.

After years of backyard experimenting, testing batches on family, friends, neighbors, and anyone willing to grab a plate, we landed on something truly out of this world. Flavors that were bold enough to stand on their own, balanced enough to elevate the natural taste of the protein, and complex enough that people kept asking: "what's in that rub?"

We bottled the answer, slapped a pig astronaut on the label, and Cosmic Pig BBQ was born — right here in Kaysville, Utah.

The Cosmic Pig Way

What We Believe

🧪

Small Batch Only

We don't mass-produce. Every batch is mixed by hand in limited quantities so quality never slips. When a batch is gone, it's gone.

🌶️

Premium Ingredients

No fillers, no anti-caking shortcuts. We source premium spices and blend them fresh so every tin hits hard from the moment you open it.

🔥

Pit-Tested

Every rub in our lineup was developed on real smokers with real fire. Lab tweaks don't cut it — if it doesn't work on a pit, it doesn't ship.

🤝

Community First

BBQ is a community. We support local retailers, local events, and the BBQ culture that makes Utah's food scene worth celebrating.
