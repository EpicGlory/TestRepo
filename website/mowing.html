What you get every visit.

🌱

The right height.

Cut at 3-4 inches in summer — the height cool-season grass actually wants. Most chains scalp lawns at 2 inches and wonder why they brown out in July.

📐

Hand-edged lines.

Driveways. Sidewalks. Flower beds. Edged with a real edger, not a string trimmer. The difference shows from the street.

🎯

String-trimmed obstacles.

Around trees, fence posts, mailboxes, AC units. No more dead grass rings where the mower can't reach.

💨

Hard surfaces blown clean.

Every driveway, walk, and patio gets blown off before we leave. Your lawn doesn't end at the grass — it ends at the curb.

🌾

Golf-course stripes.

Stripe rollers create deep alternating-direction patterns. Looks like a baseball field. Lasts about a week.

📅

Weekly or bi-weekly.

Most lawns need weekly mowing in spring/early summer, bi-weekly in mid-summer heat, weekly again in fall. We'll match the schedule to your grass.
