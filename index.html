Our Origin Story

Born from the Pit

Cosmic Pig BBQ started the way all great things do — late nights, a good fire, and an obsession with getting the flavor exactly right. After years of backyard experimentation, we landed on something truly out of this world.

Every rub in our lineup is small-batch crafted with premium spices, tested on real pits, and dialed in to work for backyard pitmasters and competition cooks alike.
