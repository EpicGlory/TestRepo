The Origin

Years of fire. One stubborn cook.

Cosmic Pig didn't start as a company — it started as a habit. Years of weekends spent over smoke, tweaking a rub blend one ingredient at a time, cooking for anyone who'd hold out a plate.

The goal was never complicated: make something people remember after the first bite, and again on the drive home. Enough people kept asking for the recipe that hiding it stopped making sense.

So here it is — the same blend, the same small batches, now with a name and a label.
