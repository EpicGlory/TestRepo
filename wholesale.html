About the Brand

Built for Bold Retail Shelves

Cosmic Pig BBQ creates small-batch BBQ rubs and seasonings in Kaysville, Utah. Our bold, retail-ready rubs are designed for BBQ shops, butcher shops, grocery stores, farmers markets, specialty food stores, and local retailers.

Seven distinct flavor profiles covering beef, pork, chicken, wings, ribs, and all-purpose use — all in shelf-ready 10–11 oz jars with full nutritional labels. Currently carried at BBQ Pit Stop locations in Layton and Lehi, Utah.
