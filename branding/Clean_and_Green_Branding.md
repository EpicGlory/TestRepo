# Clean and Green Lawn Care
## Brand Identity & Standards

---

## 1. Brand Story

Clean and Green Lawn Care was founded on a simple belief: a healthy lawn is the foundation of a healthy home. We deliver visibly cleaner, deeper-green lawns through science-backed turf programs, transparent pricing, and a relentless commitment to doing the job right the first time.

We are not a "mow-and-blow" company. We are turf specialists. Every property gets a written plan, every product is documented, every application is logged, and every customer sees exactly what they paid for and why.

---

## 2. Mission, Vision, Values

**Mission**
To give homeowners the cleanest, greenest lawn on the block through honest pricing, expert agronomy, and craftsman-level service.

**Vision**
To be the most trusted name in turf care in our service area — the company neighbors point to and ask, "Who does your lawn?"

**Core Values**
1. **Transparency** — Every quote itemized. Every product disclosed. Every price defensible.
2. **Craftsmanship** — Lawn care is a skill. We treat it like one.
3. **Stewardship** — Right product, right rate, right time. We respect the soil, the water, and the customer's family.
4. **Reliability** — We show up when we said we would, and we finish what we started.
5. **Continuous learning** — Turf science evolves. So do we.

---

## 3. Brand Positioning

**Category:** Premium residential lawn care and renovation.

**Target Customer:**
- Homeowners aged 30–65 in single-family homes with $500K+ property values
- People who care how their home looks but do not have the time, tools, or expertise to do the work themselves
- Customers frustrated with national chains that show up unannounced, apply unknown products, and bill on autopilot

**Positioning Statement**
For homeowners who want a lawn they are proud of, Clean and Green Lawn Care is the turf-care company that delivers visible, science-backed results with full transparency on every product, rate, and dollar — because unlike national franchises, we treat your lawn like our own.

**Three things we are NOT**
- Cheapest in town (we are not racing TruGreen to the bottom)
- A landscaping company (we don't install patios or do hardscape)
- A mowing service (mowing is an add-on, not the headline)

## 4. Voice & Tone

**Voice attributes**
- **Knowledgeable but plain-spoken.** We talk about pre-emergents and soil pH, but we explain them like a neighbor would.
- **Confident, not cocky.** We know what we are doing and we say so without bragging.
- **Honest.** When something will not work, we say so. When a job is more than we expected, we tell the customer before we charge them.
- **Warm.** We are local. We use first names. We remember the dog.

**Tone shifts by context**
- *Quotes/proposals:* precise, itemized, professional
- *Field communication:* friendly, clear, never condescending
- *Marketing/social:* educational, before-and-after driven, lightly humorous
- *Problem resolution:* calm, solution-first, ownership of the issue

**Words we use:** turf, program, application, agronomy, renovation, soil health, transparent, science-backed, locally owned

**Words we avoid:** "spray service," "treatments" (sounds medical), "lawn guys," anything that sounds like a chain

## 5. Visual Identity

### 5.1 Color Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary | Fairway Green | `#2E7D32` | Logo, headers, CTAs |
| Secondary | Deep Forest | `#1B4D1F` | Accents, footers |
| Accent | Fresh Cut | `#A5D6A7` | Highlights, icons |
| Neutral Dark | Charcoal | `#2C2C2C` | Body text |
| Neutral Light | Limestone | `#F4F4EF` | Backgrounds |
| Signal | Sun Yellow | `#FFC107` | Alerts, notes, "approve" buttons |

Greens dominate. Yellow appears sparingly, only where attention is required.

### 5.2 Typography

- **Display / Logo:** A confident slab or modern serif (e.g., Roboto Slab, Merriweather). Conveys craftsmanship and permanence.
- **Headings:** A geometric sans (e.g., Montserrat, Poppins) in 600/700 weight.
- **Body:** A clean humanist sans (e.g., Open Sans, Inter) at 16px minimum for proposals and 14px minimum for legal/footer.

### 5.3 Logo Direction (LOCKED)

**Primary lockup — "Mow Stripe Wordmark"**
Stacked Helvetica-900 wordmark — "CLEAN AND" in Deep Forest above an oversized "GREEN" in Fairway Green — anchored by a three-band mow-stripe baseline (Fresh Cut / Fairway / Deep Forest). Locked descriptor underneath: **TURF CARE AND RENOVATION**. Optional italic tagline ("A cleaner cut. A greener lawn.") sits below the descriptor and may be dropped at small sizes.

Reference file: `branding/logos/FINAL_logo_lockup.svg`

**Why this lockup**
- The mow-stripe band is an ownable, instantly readable visual cue tied directly to what we do
- The heavy sans wordmark reads at any scale — favicon to truck wrap
- "TURF CARE AND RENOVATION" positions us above generic "lawn guys" and makes the renovation hero service explicit on every customer touchpoint
- Two-tone green hierarchy uses the brand palette directly (no new colors)

**Usage rules**
- Minimum clear space = height of the "C" in "CLEAN" on all sides
- Never on photographic backgrounds without a contrast bar or solid plate
- Never recolored outside the approved palette
- At sizes below ~150px wide, drop the italic tagline; below ~80px wide, drop the descriptor too
- One-color versions (Fairway Green on light, White on dark) required for embroidery, signage, vehicle wraps
- Stripe order is invariant: Fresh Cut on top, Fairway Green middle, Deep Forest bottom (light → dark = depth)

### 5.4 Imagery

- Real customer lawns, real before-and-afters — never stock photography
- Wide, low-angle shots that show off stripes and color contrast
- People in branded polos, never posed
- Soft natural light; avoid mid-day sun

## 8. Customer Experience Standards

1. **Quote within 24 hours** of property walkthrough
2. **Itemized written proposal** — every product, rate, and labor hour shown
3. **Customer signature required** before any work begins
4. **Service notes after every visit** — what was applied, what we observed, what's next
5. **Photo documentation** of conditions and results
6. **30-day callback guarantee** on any application result
7. **No auto-renewals without written consent** — we earn the next season
