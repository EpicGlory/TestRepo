Cosmic Wingman

Cosmic Pig Mexican Street Corn Cups

Sweet charred corn tossed in citrus chile seasoning, creamy cotija sauce, and finished with lime. Ready in 15 minutes — no cob required.

Cook Time15 minutes
Serves4–6 (or ~16–20 small party cups)
RubCosmic Wingman
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

The Corn

  • 4 cups frozen sweet corn kernels
  • 2 Tbsp butter
  • 1–2 tsp Cosmic Wingman rub
  • ½ tsp chili powder (optional, for extra heat)

The Sauce

  • ¼ cup mayonnaise
  • ¼ cup sour cream or Mexican crema
  • ½ cup cotija cheese, crumbled
  • 1 Tbsp lime juice

Garnish

  • Extra cotija cheese
  • Extra Cosmic Wingman rub
  • Lime wedges
  • Fresh cilantro
  • Tajín or chili powder (optional)

Instructions

  1. Char the corn Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add butter, then pour in the frozen corn. Cook 8–12 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the corn is hot and lightly charred.
  2. Season it While the corn is still hot, add 1–2 tsp Cosmic Wingman. Stir well so the rub blooms into the butter and coats every kernel.
  3. Make it creamy Lower the heat. Stir in the mayo, sour cream, lime juice, and cotija cheese until everything is well coated.
  4. Taste and finish Adjust with more Cosmic Wingman, a squeeze of lime, or extra cotija to taste.
  5. Serve Spoon into cups or a bowl. Top with cotija, cilantro, a lime wedge, and a light dusting of Cosmic Wingman.
Pro Tip Want more heat? Finish with a pinch of Solar Flare or Supernova on top for a spicy kick.

Feeding a Crowd?

Scale it up for a party:

  • 5 lb frozen sweet corn
  • 1 stick butter
  • 1 cup mayo + 1 cup sour cream
  • 2 cups cotija cheese
  • ¼ cup lime juice
  • 2–3 Tbsp Cosmic Wingman

Solar Flare or Cosmic Wingman

Cosmic Pig Loaded Mac & Cheese

Rich, creamy, bacon-loaded mac & cheese with a crunchy panko topping. One pan. Two rub options. Zero leftovers.

Cook Time45 minutes (15 prep, 30 bake)
Serves8–10
RubSolar Flare or Cosmic Wingman

Pick Your Flavor

  • 🔥 Solar Flare — sweet heat chipotle — approachable, crowd-pleasing, bold
  • 🍋 Cosmic Wingman — citrus chile — bright, tangy, wing-night vibes
  • 💥 Finish with Supernova — dust the top after baking for a spicy adult version
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

The Pasta

  • 2 packages Kraft Deluxe Four Cheese Mac & Cheese
  • (cook pasta per package directions, al dente)

The Cheese Sauce

  • ½ cup butter
  • ½ cup flour
  • Up to 2 cups half-and-half or whole milk
  • ¼ cup Frank's RedHot Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp Cosmic Pig BBQ rub (Solar Flare or Cosmic Wingman)
  • 8 oz Monterey Jack cheese, freshly grated
  • 8 oz medium cheddar cheese, freshly grated
  • 6 oz real bacon bits

The Topping

  • 3 oz panko bread crumbs
  • 3 oz real bacon bits

Instructions

  1. Cook the pasta Boil the pasta until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Make the roux Melt butter in a large saucepan over medium heat. Add flour and stir continuously until the mixture turns light tan, about 2–3 minutes.
  3. Build the sauce Slowly whisk in the half-and-half. Add Frank's RedHot and 2 Tbsp of your chosen Cosmic Pig rub. Stir until smooth.
  4. Add the cheese Stir in the grated Monterey Jack and cheddar until fully melted and creamy.
  5. Add the bacon Stir 6 oz of bacon bits into the cheese sauce.
  6. Combine Fold the cheese sauce into the cooked pasta. Transfer to a 9×13 baking dish or foil pan.
  7. Top it Mix panko with the remaining 3 oz bacon bits. Sprinkle evenly over the top.
  8. Bake Bake at 350°F for 30 minutes until hot, bubbly, and golden on top.
  9. Rest and serve Let it rest 5 minutes before serving.
Pro Tip Want a spicy finish? Dust the top lightly with Supernova right out of the oven for a bold, smoky heat kick.

Smoker Option

Skip the oven and bake it on your smoker at 350°F for a light smoke layer that takes this mac to another level. Perfect for backyard BBQ and cookouts.

Feeding a Big Crowd?

Scale it up for large family gatherings, holidays, or neighborhood cookouts — makes about 25–30 servings:

  • 6 packages Kraft Deluxe Four Cheese Mac & Cheese
  • 1½ cups (3 sticks) butter
  • 1½ cups flour
  • 5–6 cups half-and-half or whole milk
  • ¾ cup Frank's RedHot Sauce
  • 5–6 Tbsp Cosmic Pig BBQ rub
  • 1½ lb Monterey Jack cheese, freshly grated
  • 1½ lb medium cheddar cheese, freshly grated
  • 18 oz real bacon bits (sauce) + 9 oz (topping)
  • 9 oz panko bread crumbs

Use a large hotel pan or two 9×13 dishes. Bake both at 350°F for 35–40 minutes.

Solar Flare

Solar Flare Candied Bacon

Thick-cut bacon glazed in brown sugar and Solar Flare rub. Sweet, sticky, and dangerously addictive — perfect on its own, crumbled over mac, or served alongside eggs.

Cook Time30 minutes
Serves4–6
RubSolar Flare
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 1 lb thick-cut bacon
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 1–2 Tbsp Solar Flare rub

Instructions

  1. Preheat Preheat oven to 375°F. Line a rimmed baking sheet with foil and place a wire rack on top.
  2. Season the bacon Lay bacon strips in a single layer on the rack. Mix the brown sugar and Solar Flare together, then sprinkle evenly over both sides of each strip.
  3. Bake Bake for 20–25 minutes until the bacon is crisp and the glaze is sticky and caramelized. Keep an eye on it in the last 5 minutes — the sugar can burn fast.
  4. Rest and serve Let the bacon cool on the rack for 5 minutes before serving. It crisps up more as it cools.
Pro Tip Want more heat? Use 2 Tbsp Solar Flare and a pinch of cayenne in the sugar mix. Great crumbled over mac & cheese, baked potatoes, or vanilla ice cream.

Feeding a Crowd?

Scale it up for a big party or family breakfast spread:

  • 3–4 lbs thick-cut bacon
  • ¾–1 cup brown sugar
  • 3–4 Tbsp Solar Flare

Bake in batches or use two oven racks at once. Rotate the pans halfway through for even caramelization.

Solar Flare

Solar Flare BBQ Meatballs

Frozen meatballs slow-cooked in a sweet, smoky Solar Flare glaze. Sticky, saucy, and gone in minutes — the easiest party food you'll ever make.

Cook Time30 minutes
Serves6–8
RubSolar Flare
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

The Meatballs

  • 2 lbs frozen meatballs (Italian, beef, or homestyle)

The Glaze

  • 1 cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup honey or brown sugar
  • 1–2 Tbsp Solar Flare rub
  • Splash of apple cider vinegar

Instructions

  1. Make the glaze Combine BBQ sauce, honey (or brown sugar), Solar Flare, and a splash of apple cider vinegar in a large saucepan or slow cooker. Stir to combine.
  2. Add the meatballs Add frozen meatballs directly into the sauce — no need to thaw.
  3. Simmer Stovetop: Cook over medium-low heat for 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until meatballs are heated through and the sauce is thick and glossy.
    Slow cooker: Cook on low for 3–4 hours or high for 1.5–2 hours.
  4. Serve Transfer to a serving dish with toothpicks. Spoon extra glaze over the top.
Pro Tip Use 2 Tbsp Solar Flare for more heat and a bolder chipotle kick. These reheat great — make them ahead and keep warm in your slow cooker throughout the party.

Feeding a Big Crowd?

Scale it up for large family gatherings or backyard parties:

  • 6–8 lbs frozen meatballs
  • 3–4 cups BBQ sauce
  • 1 cup honey or brown sugar
  • 3–4 Tbsp Solar Flare
  • 3–4 splashes apple cider vinegar

Use a large slow cooker or electric roaster to keep them warm and saucy throughout your event.

Solar Flare Sweet Heat Cream Cheese Dip

Warm, bubbly cream cheese dip with a sweet chipotle kick. Serve it with crackers, chips, or pretzels — it goes fast.

Cook Time25 minutes (10 prep, 15 bake)
Serves8–10
RubSolar Flare
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • ½ cup sour cream
  • 1–2 Tbsp Solar Flare rub
  • ½ cup shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ cup bacon bits (optional)
  • Crackers, chips, or pretzels for dipping

Instructions

  1. Preheat Preheat oven to 350°F.
  2. Mix the dip In a mixing bowl, beat together the softened cream cheese and sour cream until smooth. Stir in Solar Flare, shredded cheddar, and bacon bits if using.
  3. Bake Spread evenly into a small baking dish. Bake for 15–20 minutes until hot, bubbly, and lightly golden on top.
  4. Serve hot Serve immediately with crackers, tortilla chips, or pretzels. Top with a light dusting of extra Solar Flare for color and flavor.
Pro Tip Make it a meal by spooning this dip over baked potatoes or grilled chicken. Add jalapeños to the mix for extra heat.

Feeding a Crowd?

Double or triple it for parties and get-togethers:

  • 3 × 8 oz blocks cream cheese
  • 1½ cups sour cream
  • 3–4 Tbsp Solar Flare
  • 1½ cups shredded cheddar
  • ¾ cup bacon bits

Bake in a 9×13 dish for 25–30 minutes. Keep warm in a slow cooker on the "warm" setting.

Solar Flare Roasted Sweet Potatoes

Caramelized sweet potato cubes with a smoky sweet heat crust. A dead-simple side dish that steals the show at every BBQ spread.

Cook Time35 minutes (10 prep, 25 roast)
Serves4–6
RubSolar Flare
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs sweet potatoes, peeled and cubed into ¾-inch pieces
  • 2 Tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1–1½ Tbsp Solar Flare rub

Instructions

  1. Preheat Preheat oven to 425°F. Line a large baking sheet with foil or parchment paper.
  2. Season Toss cubed sweet potatoes with oil and Solar Flare until every piece is well coated.
  3. Roast Spread in a single layer on the baking sheet — don't crowd them or they'll steam instead of roast. Roast for 20–25 minutes, flipping once halfway through, until the edges are browned and caramelized.
  4. Serve Serve hot as a side dish. Great alongside grilled chicken, pulled pork, or any BBQ entrée.
Pro Tip For extra caramelization, crank the oven to broil for the last 2–3 minutes. A drizzle of honey right before serving takes these over the top.

Feeding a Crowd?

Scale it up for a big family cookout or neighborhood BBQ:

  • 6–8 lbs sweet potatoes
  • 6 Tbsp olive oil
  • 4–5 Tbsp Solar Flare

Use two baking sheets and rotate racks halfway through. Don't overcrowd — good browning needs airflow.

Solar Flare

Solar Flare BBQ Baked Beans

Thick, smoky baked beans loaded with bacon or pulled pork and kicked up with Solar Flare. The ultimate BBQ side dish — bake it or throw it on the smoker.

Cook Time1 hour
Serves10–12
RubSolar Flare
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 2 large cans (55 oz) baked beans (Bush's Original or similar)
  • ½ lb bacon, cooked and crumbled — or ½ lb pulled pork
  • ¼ cup BBQ sauce
  • 2 Tbsp brown sugar
  • 1–2 Tbsp Solar Flare rub

Instructions

  1. Preheat Preheat oven to 325°F (or prep your smoker to 275–300°F).
  2. Mix everything In a large cast iron skillet or oven-safe dish, combine the canned beans, cooked bacon (or pulled pork), BBQ sauce, brown sugar, and Solar Flare. Stir well.
  3. Bake or smoke Oven: Bake uncovered at 325°F for 45–60 minutes until the beans are thick and bubbling and the top has a slight glaze.
    Smoker: Smoke at 275–300°F for 1.5–2 hours for a deep smoky flavor that takes these beans to another level.
  4. Taste and serve Taste and adjust with more Solar Flare or brown sugar. Serve hot straight from the pan.
Pro Tip Pulled pork makes these extra rich and hearty. If you have leftover smoked pork from a previous cook, this is exactly where it belongs.

Smoker Option

Smoking these beans at 275–300°F for 1.5–2 hours gives them a depth of flavor that you simply can't get in an oven. Use cherry or hickory wood for the best results.

Feeding a Big Crowd?

These beans scale beautifully for large family gatherings and backyard BBQs:

  • 4 large cans baked beans
  • 1–1½ lbs bacon or pulled pork
  • ½ cup BBQ sauce
  • ¼ cup brown sugar
  • 3–4 Tbsp Solar Flare

Use a large foil hotel pan and bake at 325°F for 60–75 minutes, or smoke for 2–2.5 hours. Stir once halfway through.

Exploding Uranus ☠️

Ghost Pepper Wings

Crispy dry-rubbed wings with a ghost pepper bark that builds slow and hits hard. No sauce — just heat, smoke, and crunch. Not for the timid.

Cook Time60 minutes (10 prep, 50 bake)
Serves4–6
RubExploding Uranus
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs chicken wings, split into flats and drums
  • 1 Tbsp baking powder (not baking soda — this is the crispy secret)
  • 1½–2 Tbsp Exploding Uranus rub
  • 2 Tbsp butter, melted
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Lime wedges for serving

Instructions

  1. Dry the wings Pat wings completely dry with paper towels. The drier the wings, the crispier the skin.
  2. Season Toss wings with baking powder and Exploding Uranus until evenly coated. Place on a wire rack over a baking sheet. For best results, refrigerate uncovered 1–4 hours (or overnight).
  3. Bake Preheat oven to 425°F. Bake wings for 45–50 minutes, flipping once at the 25-minute mark, until the skin is crispy and deeply browned.
  4. Finish While wings are still hot, toss in melted butter mixed with minced garlic. The butter carries the heat deeper into the wing.
  5. Serve Serve immediately with lime wedges and a cold drink. Pair with Cosmic Wingman Sour Cream Dip to tame the heat.
Pro Tip Want smoker wings? Run them at 275°F for 45 minutes, then crank to 425°F (or move to a hot grill) for 10 minutes to crisp the skin. Best of both worlds.

Feeding a Crowd?

  • 8–10 lbs chicken wings
  • 3 Tbsp baking powder
  • 4–5 Tbsp Exploding Uranus
  • 4 Tbsp butter + 4 cloves garlic

Use two oven racks and rotate halfway through. Don't crowd the pan — airflow is what makes them crispy.

Exploding Uranus ☠️

Apocalyptic Pulled Pork

Low and slow smoked pork shoulder with an Exploding Uranus bark that is equal parts beautiful and brutal. Sweet smoke up front, ghost pepper heat on the way down. Worth every hour.

Cook Time8–10 hours
Serves10–14
RubExploding Uranus
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

The Pork

  • 8–10 lb bone-in pork shoulder (butt)
  • 2–3 Tbsp yellow mustard (binder)
  • 3–4 Tbsp Exploding Uranus rub

The Spritz

  • ½ cup apple cider vinegar
  • ½ cup apple juice

To Serve

  • Brioche buns or slider rolls
  • Creamy coleslaw (cooling contrast to the heat)
  • Extra Exploding Uranus for dusting

Instructions

  1. Prep the pork Trim excess fat cap to about ¼ inch. Coat the entire shoulder with a thin layer of yellow mustard — this acts as a binder and adds zero mustard flavor once cooked.
  2. Apply the rub Generously coat all sides with Exploding Uranus. Press it in. Let it rest at room temperature 30–60 minutes before cooking, or refrigerate overnight for deeper flavor.
  3. Smoke it Set your smoker to 250°F. Place the pork fat-side up and smoke for 5–6 hours, spritzing with the apple cider vinegar + apple juice mixture every 60–90 minutes after the first 2 hours.
  4. Wrap it When the internal temp hits 165°F and the bark is set (dark, firm, and mahogany), wrap tightly in butcher paper or foil. Return to the smoker.
  5. Take it home Continue cooking until the internal temp reaches 200–205°F and a probe slides in like butter — no resistance. Total cook time is 8–10 hours.
  6. Rest and pull Rest wrapped in a cooler (or warm oven at 170°F) for at least 1 hour. Pull apart with two forks or bear claws. Serve on buns with cold coleslaw.
Pro Tip The coleslaw isn't optional — the cool, creamy crunch is the perfect contrast to the ghost pepper heat. A drizzle of Supernova Chipotle Fry Sauce on top takes it over the edge.

Wood Choice

Cherry or apple wood for a slightly sweet smoke that complements the brown sugar in the rub. Hickory if you want bold and smoky. Avoid mesquite — too aggressive with this rub.

Exploding Uranus ☠️

Hellfire Smash Burger

Ghost pepper smash burger with a hard sear, melted pepper jack, and a spread of Supernova Chipotle Fry Sauce. Sweet, smoky, and dangerously spicy. You've been warned.

Cook Time15 minutes
Serves4
RubExploding Uranus
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

The Burgers

  • 1½ lbs 80/20 ground beef, divided into 4–8 loose balls (2 smash patties per burger)
  • 1–2 tsp Exploding Uranus rub per ball
  • 4 slices pepper jack cheese
  • Salt to taste

The Build

  • 4 brioche buns, toasted
  • Supernova Chipotle Fry Sauce (see recipe)
  • Dill pickle slices
  • Thinly sliced white onion

Instructions

  1. Heat the pan Get a cast iron skillet or flat griddle screaming hot over high heat. No oil needed — the fat in the beef handles it.
  2. Season and smash Season each beef ball with Exploding Uranus. Place in the hot pan and immediately smash flat with a burger press or heavy spatula. Hold pressure for 10 seconds. Season the top side.
  3. Sear and cheese Cook 2–3 minutes until the edges are deeply browned and crispy. Flip once, immediately lay pepper jack on top, and cook 1 more minute.
  4. Build the burger Spread Supernova Chipotle Fry Sauce on the toasted bun. Stack the cheesy patties, add pickles and onion, and close it up.
Pro Tip Don't move the patty after smashing — let the crust form. The crispy seared edges are everything. Stack two thinner patties per burger for maximum surface area and crunch.

Exploding Uranus ☠️

Brave Soul Shrimp Skewers

Ghost pepper shrimp that caramelize fast on a hot grill and hit with wave after wave of heat. Ready in 15 minutes. Best paired with something cold and someone who can handle the heat.

Cook Time15 minutes (10 prep, 5 grill)
Serves4
RubExploding Uranus
VIEW RECIPE ↓

Ingredients

  • 1½ lbs large shrimp (16/20 count), peeled and deveined, tails on
  • 1½–2 tsp Exploding Uranus rub
  • 3 Tbsp butter, melted
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • Skewers (metal, or wooden soaked in water 30 min)
  • Fresh parsley or cilantro for garnish

Instructions

  1. Make the garlic butter Combine melted butter, minced garlic, lemon zest, and half the lemon juice. Set aside.
  2. Season the shrimp Toss shrimp with Exploding Uranus and half the garlic butter until evenly coated. Skewer 4–5 shrimp per skewer, threading through the tail and body.
  3. Grill hot and fast Preheat grill to high heat (450–500°F). Grill skewers 2–3 minutes per side until shrimp are pink, slightly charred, and just cooked through. Don't overcook — shrimp go rubbery fast.
  4. Finish and serve Pull off the grill and immediately brush with the remaining garlic butter. Squeeze the remaining lemon juice over the top, garnish with parsley or cilantro, and serve at once.
Pro Tip These are great over rice, tucked into tacos with Cosmic Wingman Sour Cream Dip, or served as an appetizer skewer. The ghost pepper heat is intense but balanced by the butter and lemon — it builds the longer you eat.
