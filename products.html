The Lineup

Our Signature Rubs

Four small-batch rubs, each dialed in for different flavor profiles and proteins. Mix, match, and layer them for truly cosmic results.

✨ Mild

Cosmic Dust

The everyday all-star. Herbaceous and savory with a hint of garlic and onion, Cosmic Dust brings balance to any protein without overpowering the natural flavor of the meat.

Heat:
Chicken Pork Chops Fish Vegetables Turkey

Flavor Notes: Garlic · Onion · Rosemary · Sea Salt · Black Pepper

Best With: Great as an all-purpose seasoning — works on the grill, in the oven, or in the smoker.

⭐ Medium

Nebula Blend

Sweet brown sugar and smoky paprika orbit tangy mustard powder in a rub that was born for pork. The Nebula Blend builds a mahogany bark unlike anything else.

Heat:
Pork Ribs Pork Butt Pulled Pork Ham

Flavor Notes: Brown Sugar · Smoked Paprika · Mustard · Cinnamon · Cayenne

Best With: Low-and-slow pork. Apply generously the night before for maximum bark development.

🌟 Hot

Solar Flare

Cayenne, chipotle, and habanero converge in a fiery rub designed for those who like their BBQ with serious heat. The smoke note keeps it from being just heat — it's complex fire.

Heat:
Brisket Burgers Steak Wings

Flavor Notes: Cayenne · Chipotle · Habanero · Smoked Salt · Cumin

Best With: Beef and chicken — anywhere you want serious heat with layers of smoke flavor.

🌑 Bold

Dark Matter

Espresso, Dutch cocoa, and cracked black pepper merge into a rub so deep it bends reality. Dark Matter is the rub for competition-level brisket and whole smoked beef ribs.

Heat:
Brisket Beef Short Rib Lamb Venison

Flavor Notes: Espresso · Dutch Cocoa · Black Pepper · Ancho · Kosher Salt

Best With: Beef and lamb — the coffee and cocoa create an extraordinary bark on long, slow cooks.

Pitmaster Tips

How to Apply

Getting the most out of your Cosmic Pig rubs is easy when you follow a few key principles.

⏱️

Apply Early

For maximum flavor, apply your rub 1–12 hours before cooking. Overnight is ideal for larger cuts like brisket and pork butt.

🖐️

Be Generous

Don't be shy — coat all surfaces evenly, pressing the rub in so it adheres. A generous coat builds that signature crust.

🌡️

Let It Ride

Patience is the secret ingredient. Trust the process — low and slow smoke lets the rub caramelize into a perfect bark.

🧪

Mix & Layer

Don't be afraid to combine rubs. A base coat of Cosmic Dust + a top layer of Solar Flare = otherworldly wings.
